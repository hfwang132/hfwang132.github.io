Before I knew it, I had been in Singapore for almost a year and a half. I did not expect that the last time I wrote a diary entry was also a year and a half ago.

Can this still be called a diary? It is more like an “annual journal”!

Actually, I do keep a diary, but it is entirely just a running account, recording only what I did and what work I completed that day, so that it is convenient to report during group meetings.

It seems this really is an “annual journal.”

Where should I begin... Perhaps I can write it by time period?

### Aug - Oct 2023

During this period, I was basically studying. I crammed knowledge of quantum optics and quantum information, and chatted with senior lab members to learn some experiment-related knowledge (such as g2 and integrated CNOT gates).

I also had classes to attend.

### Oct - Dec 2023 

I finally started doing experiments in mid-October! First, I learned the previous senior lab member's setup, then aligned the optical path together with the postdoc. I also wrote a lot of code to control the timetagger and translation stages.

Then I went to Japan for a trip!

### Jan - Feb 2024

Worked on a new project with the postdoc! But first, I familiarized myself with the 4-photon setup, including obtaining the HOM dip.

I took a break for a while during the Lunar New Year.

### Mar - Apr 2024

Built the optical path and wrote code; the main work was all completed.

I also had classes to attend.

### May - Jul 2024

Since all the experimental work was finished, I had nothing much to do for the time being, so I read papers intensively and studied.

Of course, I occasionally needed to make up for some things on the theoretical side, so I also had to write some code for processing.

During this period, I wanted to restore the 4-photon setup. Unsuccessful. But I accumulated a great deal of experience.

Then I went to Korea for a trip.

### Jul - Sep 2024

After returning from Korea, I tinkered around for a while and picked up many hobbies again.

Then I completely dismantled and rebuilt the setup, figuring out many issues.

### Oct - Nov 2024

Aligned the old setup, prepared items and arrangements for the new lab, then went to Beijing with my supervisor for a meeting. After returning, I built the setup for the new project.

### Dec 2024 - Jan 2025

During this period, I studied the FPGA used for the new project. In between, I traveled to the UK.

**Damn! How did this turn into a running account again!**
